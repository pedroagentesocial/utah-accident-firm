# PRODUCT — Utah Accident Firm

register: brand

## Product purpose
Marketing landing site for **utahaccidentfirm.com**, a Utah company that handles
everything after a car accident: filing and managing the auto **insurance claim**,
fighting for a **fair payout**, and **coordinating the vehicle repair**.

**They are NOT a law firm.** They do not give legal advice or representation. They
are insurance-claim managers and repair coordinators. A visible legal disclaimer
stating this is a hard requirement, not optional.

## Users
Someone who **just had a car accident and is stressed**, often on their phone at
the roadside. Many are Spanish-speaking Utah residents. They want relief, not
jargon: "someone competent just took this off my plate." Secondary: people days
after a crash, comparing options, unsure if they need a lawyer.

## Tone & voice
- Relief and reassurance: "Nosotros nos encargamos." Calm, human, plain language.
- No legal/insurance jargon. No fear-mongering. No fake urgency.
- Bilingual: English + **US Spanish (es-US, Latino-neutral)**. Never es-ES, no
  "vosotros". Dollars, Utah area codes (801/385), real Utah cities.

## Strategic principles
- One job: get contact (Call / WhatsApp / form) from any scroll position.
- Mobile-first; most visitors arrive from a phone right after a crash.
- Honesty: never invent testimonials, reviews, or statistics. Real figures only;
  otherwise show clearly-labeled placeholders or hide the section.
- The "not a law firm" disclaimer stays visible (footer + where relevant).

## Anti-references / anti-goals
- The generic "insurance/accident = trustworthy navy blue + stock gavel" template.
- Anything that reads as a law firm (no scales of justice, gavels, "attorney").
- Fabricated 5-star reviews or invented case numbers.
- Cold, corporate, intimidating. This brand is warm and human.
